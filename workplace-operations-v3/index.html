Management decisions & project approach

Why these KPIs?

Budget variance identifies resource overruns and early intervention points. Utilization indicates pressure on funding. Vendor composite scores provide consistent, auditable evaluations across service partners. Scores trigger reviews, not automatic termination.

Operating cadence

  1. Weekly: review work-order response and resolution SLA exceptions, contractor delays, and emerging spend.
  2. Monthly: review three-way invoice exceptions, reconcile payments against approved records, compare spend with plan, and document variance explanations.
  3. Quarterly: review scorecards with vendors and agree on corrective actions with dated follow-up.
